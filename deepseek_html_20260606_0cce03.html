Regenerative materials · Muqarnas logic · Passive stone

The ethics of stone & mirror

Regenerative Materials: Polished calcite and marble are not just surfaces — they are geological archives. When quarried responsibly and paired with low-carbon binders, stone offers centuries of durability and full recyclability. The Mirror Mosques taught us that light reflected through faceted stone becomes a spiritual tool. We apply that principle to modern cladding: low-embodied carbon, locally sourced where possible, and designed for disassembly.

Muqarnas as Algorithm: The stalactite vault is a fractal logic written in stone and plaster. Every niche, every mirror facet multiplies light infinitely. At EXO Studio, we translate muqarnas tiling into parametric workflows — generating faceted cladding systems that breathe, reflect, and cool passively.

Architect as light alchemist

EXO Studio studies the Mirror Mosques: their muqarnas ceilings turn humble plaster into infinite reflections. We translate that into computational workflows, BIM objects, and open-source texture libraries. Every calcite vein, every marble swirl carries the memory of deep time.

We work with architects who want their digital models to shimmer, their renderings to feel like polished stone, and their buildings to one day return to the earth — or be reborn as new facades.
